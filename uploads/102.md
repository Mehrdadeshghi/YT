# Wiki Roulette #102 · The Maracanazo, 1950 (Football's Craziest Moments · Part 4)
**Title:** They were "world champions"… BEFORE the game 😳
**Alt:** Brazil printed the headline. Then Uruguay happened.

**Description:**
Worst defeat in World Cup history? Comment YES or NO 👇

16 July 1950, Maracanã, Rio de Janeiro. In the last match of the World Cup, Brazil only needed a draw against Uruguay. Gold medals had already been made with each player's name, a victory song was composed and rehearsed, and the newspaper O Mundo printed an early edition with a photo of the team captioned "These are the world champions". 173,850 fans (official count) watched Friaça put Brazil ahead in the 47th minute — then Schiaffino (66') and Ghiggia (79') scored for Uruguay. The stadium fell into "disturbing and traumatic absolute silence". Brazil's white shirts were criticised afterwards, and the yellow kit was chosen in a 1953 design contest.
