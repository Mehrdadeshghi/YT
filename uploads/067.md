The black seadevil (humpback anglerfish) lives 200–1,500 m down. Females hunt with a glowing lure whose light comes from bacteria, and their stretchy stomach lets them eat meals heavier than themselves. Females reach about 15 cm; males only 1.5–2.8 cm. On 5 February 2025, a living female was filmed at the surface in daylight, about 2 km off Tenerife.
