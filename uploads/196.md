# #196 REEL v2 (E8) — Why Hoover Dam is full of pipes

**Title:** Hoover Dam would have taken 125 years to cool 🧊
**Alt title:** 125 years of cooling, cut to 2. The trick inside Hoover Dam #shorts

**Description:**
Concrete heats up as it cures. Engineers calculated that if Hoover Dam had been poured in one single continuous pour, the concrete would have taken 125 years to cool, and the stresses would have cracked the dam.
So it was poured in blocks (columns), some up to 50 ft square and 5 ft high. Every five-foot block got 1-inch steel pipes: first cool river water ran through them, then ice-cold water from a refrigeration plant. When a block stopped shrinking, the pipes were filled with grout. More than 582 miles of cooling pipe are hidden inside the dam. Pouring took about two years (June 6, 1933 – May 29, 1935).
