Hans Island is a 1.3 km² rock in the Kennedy Channel between Canada's Ellesmere Island and Greenland. In 1984 Canada left a flag and a bottle of Canadian whisky; Denmark's minister answered with a Danish flag and a bottle of schnapps. For decades both sides took turns planting flags and leaving bottles. On 14 June 2022 the two countries split the island nearly in half along a natural rift.
