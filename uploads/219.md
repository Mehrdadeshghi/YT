**Title:** Why you feel your phone buzz when it didn't 📱
**Alt title:** Phantom vibration syndrome: 68% of hospital staff felt it #shorts

**Description:**
Your phone didn't buzz, but you felt it anyway. It has a name: phantom vibration syndrome. In a survey of medical staff at a US academic medical centre, 68% (115 of 169) had experienced phantom vibrations (Rothberg et al., BMJ 2010). Researcher Michelle Drouin found that almost 9 out of 10 undergraduates at her college had experienced them. They typically start after carrying a phone for between one month and one year. The cause is not known; one suggestion is that a brain expecting a call misreads other sensations, like a muscle twitch or the pressure of clothing, as a vibration. Turning off vibrate or carrying the phone in a different place helped some people.
