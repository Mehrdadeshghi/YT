# Wiki Roulette #032 · Wojtek (bear)
**Title:** This bear was a soldier. A corporal.
**Alt:** The bear who carried artillery shells

**Description:**
In 1942 Polish soldiers in Iran adopted an orphaned Syrian brown bear cub and named him Wojtek. He drank beer, marched on two legs and slept next to the soldiers. To keep him, he was officially enlisted as a private in the 22nd Artillery Supply Company. At the Battle of Monte Cassino he carried 45 kg crates of artillery shells and never dropped one. He was promoted to corporal, and a bear carrying a shell became the unit's emblem. He later lived at Edinburgh Zoo until 1963.
