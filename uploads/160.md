# #160 OCEAN — One punch, two hits (mantis shrimp)

**Title:** This shrimp hits everything twice 🥊
**Alt title:** The mantis shrimp punches at 51 mph — and every punch hits twice #shorts

**Description:**
The peacock mantis shrimp swings its club at 51 mph from a standing start. The strike is so fast that it creates vapor bubbles in the water (cavitation), and when they collapse they hit the prey a second time. Even if it misses, the shock wave can stun or kill, and the collapsing bubbles even give off light. And its eyes? Some mantis shrimp have up to 16 types of light receptors. You have four.
