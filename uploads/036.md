# Wiki Roulette #036 · Vesna Vulović
**Title:** She fell 10 km. No parachute.
**Alt:** She survived a 10,160 m fall

**Description:**
On 26 January 1972, 22-year-old flight attendant Vesna Vulović was aboard JAT Flight 367 when a bomb exploded in the baggage compartment and the plane broke apart. She fell 10,160 metres and was the only survivor of 28 people, with a fractured skull, broken legs and broken vertebrae. In 1985 Guinness World Records listed it as the highest fall survived without a parachute. (In 2009 some journalists disputed the altitude; aviation authorities rejected the claim.)
