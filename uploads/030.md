# Wiki Roulette #030 · Tsutomu Yamaguchi
**Title:** He survived Hiroshima. Then Nagasaki.
**Alt:** The man who survived both atomic bombs

**Description:**
On 6 August 1945, Tsutomu Yamaguchi was in Hiroshima on a business trip for Mitsubishi, about 3 km from ground zero. The blast burst his eardrums and left him with serious burns. The next day he returned home to Nagasaki, and on 9 August he was describing Hiroshima to his supervisor when the second bomb exploded. He survived again. In 2009 Japan officially recognised him as a survivor of both bombings. He died in 2010, aged 93.
