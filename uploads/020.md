## Beschreibung
In 1965, a French notary made a deal with 90-year-old Jeanne Calment: he would pay her 2,500 francs every month, and when she died, her apartment in Arles would be his. He paid for 30 years. Then, on 25 December 1995, he died first. By then she had received more than double the apartment's value. Jeanne Calment lived to 122 years and 164 days, the oldest verified person in history. As a teenager she met Vincent van Gogh. Her review: "very disagreeable."
