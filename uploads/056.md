**Title:** 3 minutes here could kill you
**Alt:** Chernobyl's Elephant's Foot

**Description:**

The Elephant's Foot is a mass of corium – melted nuclear fuel mixed with sand and concrete – beneath reactor 4 at Chernobyl. When it was found in December 1986, eight months after the disaster, it emitted up to 10,000 roentgens per hour: a lethal dose in about 3 minutes. Its famous 1996 photos were taken by Artur Korneyev with an automatic camera. It is weaker today, but still dangerous.
