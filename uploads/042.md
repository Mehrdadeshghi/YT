# Wiki Roulette #042 · Emperor Norton
**Title:** He declared himself Emperor of the USA
**Alt:** San Francisco's self-made emperor

**Description:**
After losing his fortune trying to corner the rice market, Joshua Norton handed a San Francisco newspaper a proclamation on 17 September 1859 declaring himself 'Emperor of these United States'. Emperor Norton I ordered Congress dissolved, which it ignored. San Francisco played along, and some establishments accepted the notes he issued. Among his decrees was a bridge across San Francisco Bay, which exists today as the Bay Bridge.
