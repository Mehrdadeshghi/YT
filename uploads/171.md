# 171 OCEAN — This snail hunts fish with a harpoon (cone snail)

**Title:** This snail hunts fish. With a harpoon 🎯
**Alt title:** The cone snail fires a venom harpoon at 56 mph #shorts

**Description:**
Cone snails turned a tooth into a hollow, barbed harpoon, loaded with venom and fired from a long tube. In one species the harpoon reaches up to 56 mph, one of the fastest movements ever measured in an animal. About 30 people have been killed by cone snails, and the harpoon can sting through a wetsuit. The geography cone even uses insulin as a weapon: nearby fish go into shock. And doctors turned cone snail venom (from the magician cone, Conus magus) into a painkiller, ziconotide, approved by the U.S. FDA in 2004.
