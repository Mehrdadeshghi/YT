# Wiki Roulette #069 · Alpheidae (fact-photo special)
**Title:** This shrimp hunts with a bubble.
**Alt:** The pistol shrimp's bubble gun

**Description:**

Pistol shrimp snap their oversized claw so fast that they shoot out a bubble at 25 m/s (90 km/h). When it collapses, it makes a 218 dB sound (underwater, re 1 μPa) that can stun fish, and even a flash of light: if that light were thermal, it would take over 5,000 K, close to the Sun's surface at 5,772 K. Colonies can interfere with sonar. Some species live with a goby that keeps watch while the shrimp keeps an antenna on it. One species, Synalpheus pinkfloydi (2017), was named after Pink Floyd for its pink claw.
