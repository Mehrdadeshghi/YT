**Title:** Yellowstone is NOT overdue 🌋
**Alt title:** Is the Yellowstone supervolcano overdue? What the USGS actually says #shorts

**Description:**
The Yellowstone Caldera is about 45 by 28 miles (72 by 45 km). The volcanic field had three huge caldera-forming eruptions: about 2.1 million, 1.3 million and 640,000 years ago. The last one, the Lava Creek eruption, threw out about 240 cubic miles (1,000 km³) of rock, dust and volcanic ash. The time between the last three cataclysmic eruptions ranged from 600,000 to 800,000 years — but so few eruptions cannot be used to predict the next one. The Yellowstone Volcano Observatory (USGS, University of Utah, National Park Service) says Yellowstone is not "overdue" for a super-eruption and does not consider an eruption imminent. A 2013 analysis found that only 6–8% of the magma chamber is molten; scientists believe that is too low for another super-eruption.
