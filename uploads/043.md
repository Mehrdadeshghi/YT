# Wiki Roulette #043 · Operation Mincemeat
**Title:** A dead man fooled Hitler
**Alt:** The corpse that changed WW2

**Description:**
In 1943 British intelligence dressed the body of Glyndwr Michael as a fictitious officer, Major William Martin of the Royal Marines, carrying fake letters saying the Allies would invade Greece and Sardinia instead of Sicily. The submarine HMS Seraph released the body off Huelva, Spain. German messages showed they believed the deception; Hitler sent forces to Greece. On 9 July 1943 the Allies invaded Sicily.
