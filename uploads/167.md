There's a stonefish hiding in this photo, the most venomous fish known, and it looks exactly like a rock. It has 13 spines on its back, each with two venom sacs, and they can go right through a boot sole. It can survive out of the water for up to 24 hours, so people even get stung on the beach. And yet people eat it raw, as sashimi: cut off the spiny fin and it's harmless.
