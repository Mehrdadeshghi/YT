In October 1961 the Soviet Union detonated the Tsar Bomba over Novaya Zemlya: about 50 megatons, roughly 1,570 times the combined energy of the Hiroshima and Nagasaki bombs. The fireball was about 8 km wide and the mushroom cloud rose to 67 km. Windows broke up to 900 km away. And it was a toned-down version – the design allowed for about 100 megatons.
