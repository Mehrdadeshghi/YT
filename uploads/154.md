# #154 CIRCLES #3 — How many circles does it take to draw Florida?

**Title:** How many circles does it take to draw Florida? 🌴
**Alt title:** 1 circle misses Florida by 170 miles. 500 circles? #shorts

**Description:**
Spinning circles, each riding on the last one, can draw any shape (a Fourier series). With 1 circle, Florida is off by up to 170 miles. With 500 circles, it's off by just 1 mile. Watch Pensacola, the Big Bend, Tampa Bay and Miami appear one by one.
