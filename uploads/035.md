# Wiki Roulette #035 · Hiroo Onoda
**Title:** The war ended in 1945. He kept fighting.
**Alt:** 28 years of a war that was already over

**Description:**
Japanese soldier Hiroo Onoda hid in the jungle of Lubang Island in the Philippines after World War II ended. Leaflets told him Japan had surrendered, but he believed they were enemy propaganda. He held out for 28 years and 6 months (10,416 days). In 1974 the adventurer Norio Suzuki found him, but Onoda still refused to surrender until his former commander, Major Yoshimi Taniguchi, flew in and relieved him of duty on 9 March 1974.
