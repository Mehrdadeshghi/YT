**Title:** The Statue of Liberty was born brown 🗽
**Alt title:** Congress almost painted the Statue of Liberty #shorts

**Description:**
When the Statue of Liberty was dedicated on October 28, 1886, she was reddish-brown and shiny: her skin is copper, only 0.094 inches (2.4 mm) thick. Reactions with air, water and acidic pollution slowly turned it into a green patina (verdigris). It was mentioned in the press as early as 1902, and by 1906 it covered the whole statue.
Believing the patina was evidence of corrosion, Congress authorized $62,800 for repairs and to paint the statue inside and out. There was considerable public protest against the exterior painting, and the Army Corps of Engineers concluded that the patina protected the skin and "softened the outlines of the Statue and made it beautiful." The statue was painted only on the inside.
