**Alt title:** The fish that looks exactly like seaweed (leafy seadragon) #shorts

**Description:**
This is not seaweed. It's a fish: the leafy seadragon, a cousin of the seahorse. Those leaves are lobes of skin, used only for camouflage. Tiny, almost completely transparent fins push it along, so it drifts just like floating seaweed. And the dads carry the eggs: the female lays up to 250 bright pink eggs onto the male's tail. About nine weeks later they hatch, and only about 5% of the eggs survive.
