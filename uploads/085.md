Germany's famous "Hundertjähriger Kalender" (100-year calendar) predicts the first snow on December 1, 2026, and more snow on December 5. Frost from November 16. A white Christmas? Probably not: December 20–28 mostly rain, snow only at higher elevations. From December 29 to New Year's Eve heavy snow and icy cold, and January almost entirely bitterly cold.
It goes back to Abbot Mauritius Knauer, who recorded the weather at Langheim Abbey in Franconia, Germany, for seven years from around 1652. His idea: seven "planets" each rule one year — 2026 is a Mercury year. Meteorologists say the predictions don't hold up; any hits are considered chance. This is not a weather forecast.
