**Title:** 5000 to 1. They won the league. 🦊
**Alt title:** The greatest upset in sports? Leicester City 2015-16 #shorts

**Description:**
Before the 2015-16 season, bookmakers made Leicester City 5000-1 underdogs to win the Premier League. The season before, they had spent four and a half months at the bottom of the table (late November to mid-April) before escaping relegation. Then Jamie Vardy scored in 11 consecutive Premier League games, a new record (previously Ruud van Nistelrooy). Leicester finished with 81 points: 23 wins, 12 draws, only 3 defeats. On 2 May 2016 Chelsea came back from 2-0 down to draw 2-2 with Tottenham at Stamford Bridge, and Leicester were champions of England for the first time in the club's history.
