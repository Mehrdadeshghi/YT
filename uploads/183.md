# #183 FOOTBALL — Not even in the tournament. Then they won it. (Denmark, Euro 1992)

**Title:** 10 days before the Euros, they weren't even in it. Then they won it. 🇩🇰
**Alt title:** The team that wasn't supposed to be there: Denmark, Euro 1992 #shorts

**Description:**
Ten days before Euro 1992 in Sweden, Denmark had not qualified. Yugoslavia had won the qualifying group, but on 31 May 1992 they were thrown out because of the breakup of the country and the war there. Denmark, runners-up in the group, took the spot with nine days to prepare. (The famous story that the players were called back from the beach is a myth: most of them were already together for a friendly.) Group stage: 0-0 against England, 0-1 against hosts Sweden, then 2-1 against France. Semi-final against the reigning European champions, the Netherlands: 2-2, and in the shoot-out Peter Schmeichel saved Marco van Basten's penalty, 5-4. Final on 26 June 1992 at the Nya Ullevi in Gothenburg against world champions Germany: 2-0, goals by John Jensen and Kim Vilfort. Denmark's first European title.
