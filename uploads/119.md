# Wiki Roulette #119 · BODY FACTS #1: CPR: the song that can save a life
**Title:** This song can save a life ❤️🎵
**Alt:** Push to the beat of Stayin' Alive 🕺

**Description:**
Would you know what to do? Comment YES or NO 👇

When the heart suddenly stops, every minute counts. In the US, only about 1 in 10 people survive a cardiac arrest outside a hospital. Immediate CPR can double or even triple the chance of survival (American Heart Association). Here's how: 1) Call emergency services (112 in Europe, 911 in the US). 2) Put your hands in the middle of the chest. 3) Push hard, about 5 cm (2 inches) deep, and fast: 100 to 120 times per minute. The trick: "Stayin' Alive" by the Bee Gees has about 104 beats per minute, the perfect rhythm. Keep going until help arrives. Even astronauts on the ISS train CPR. Learn it in a first-aid course!

ⓘ General information, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 112 (Europe) or 911 (US).

Quand le cœur s'arrête brusquement, chaque minute compte. Aux États-Unis, seule environ 1 personne sur 10 survit à un arrêt cardiaque hors de l'hôpital. Même les astronautes de l'ISS s'entraînent au massage cardiaque. Apprends-le lors d'une formation aux premiers secours !
