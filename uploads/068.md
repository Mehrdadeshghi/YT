The colossal squid has the largest eyes of any known creature ever, an estimated 27–30 cm across. The heaviest specimen, caught in 2007, weighed 495 kg and is on display at Te Papa in New Zealand. Its arms and tentacles carry hooks, some of them swivelling. Sperm whales hunt it: 14% of the squid beaks in Antarctic sperm whale stomachs are colossal squid. The first confirmed footage of a living one was filmed in March 2025: a juvenile about 30 cm long, 600 m deep.
