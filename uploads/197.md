**Description:**
The sperm whale makes clicks with a source level of up to 236 decibels underwater, the loudest of any animal. It dives as deep as 7,380 feet (2,250 m) for up to two hours, where no sunlight reaches. It "sees" with sound: powerful clicks from its huge head (up to one third of its length), and the echoes come back through its lower jaw. It recycles its air, so it can keep clicking for the whole dive. Tagging studies show it hunts upside down, for squid, even giant squid.
