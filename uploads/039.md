# Wiki Roulette #039 · Point Nemo
**Title:** The closest humans here are in space
**Alt:** The loneliest spot on Earth

**Description:**
Point Nemo is the oceanic pole of inaccessibility, the point in the ocean farthest from any land, about 2,688 km from the nearest islands. Often the closest humans are astronauts on the International Space Station passing roughly 400 km overhead. The area is also a spacecraft cemetery, where decommissioned spacecraft are brought down, and the ISS is planned to end there in 2031.
