**Title:** 3-0 down at half-time. They still won. 🤯
**Alt title:** The greatest comeback in Champions League history? Istanbul 2005 #shorts

**Description:**
Champions League final, Istanbul, 25 May 2005. Milan scored in the first minute (Maldini), Crespo added two more, and Liverpool went in 3-0 down at half-time. Then Liverpool scored three goals in six minutes: Gerrard, Šmicer, Alonso. 3-3. In the 117th minute Dudek saved twice from Shevchenko, the second time from under 3 yards. In the shoot-out he saved from Pirlo and Shevchenko, and Liverpool won 3-2 on penalties.
