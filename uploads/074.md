Enypniastes eximia, nicknamed the headless chicken monster or Spanish dancer, is a deep-sea cucumber that swims. Its body is semi-transparent so you can see its gut, it rows with a sail-like front, it is 11–25 cm long and can be bioluminescent. In 2018 a camera captured one off East Antarctica, the first sighting in the Southern Ocean. Footage: NOAA Okeanos Explorer, Gulf of Mexico, 2014.
