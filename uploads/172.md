**Description:**
The sarcastic fringehead (Neoclinus blanchardi) lives off the coast of California, from San Francisco down to Baja California. It is only 6 to 10 inches long, but when a male flares its jaws, its mouth can open to as much as four times its closed size, brightly colored inside. It backs into shells, burrows, bottles and even soda cans and shows only its head. Two males settle a fight by pressing their open mouths together until one bites the other; the loser has to find a new home. Divers say these little fish can even damage their wetsuits.
