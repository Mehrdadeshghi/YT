**Title:** Why is this the "doomsday fish"? 🐉
**Alt title:** The 23-foot fish that washed up before Japan's 2011 earthquake #shorts

**Description:**
The giant oarfish is the longest bony fish on Earth. The one in the photo, found near San Diego in 1996, was 23 feet long. Healthy ones are rarely seen, and many oarfish washed up in Japan before the 2011 earthquake and tsunami, which is why people call it the "doomsday fish". But there is no real link: the idea that it predicts earthquakes isn't supported. The truly weird part: it cuts off its own tail, again and again. Its organs sit near the head, so it survives.
