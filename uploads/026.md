# Wiki Roulette #026 · Dord
**Title:** This fake word was in the dictionary
**Alt:** The word that never existed

**Description:**
In 1931, a note read "D or d, cont./density", meaning a capital or small D can stand for density. Because headwords were typed with spaces between the letters, it was misread as one word: "dord". It was printed in the 1934 second edition of Merriam's New International Dictionary. In 1939 an editor noticed it had no etymology, and by 1947 it was gone.
