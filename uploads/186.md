# #186 WHY? #1 — Why are pigeon nests so bad?

**Title:** Why are pigeon nests so bad? 🪺
**Alt title:** Weavers build masterpieces. Pigeons throw down sticks. Why? #shorts

**Description:**
Weaver birds weave hanging nests; a pigeon nest is, in Wikipedia's words, "a flimsy platform of straw and sticks". Three reasons:
1) Pigeons are cliff birds. Wild rock doves nest on ledges and in crevices of sea cliffs, canyons and caves, under cover of the rock. Human buildings imitate those cliffs, which is why skyscrapers, highway overpasses and farm buildings are full of pigeon nests.
2) No show-off. A village weaver male tears off about 300 long leaf strips, one by one, and for weavers the nest is part of the courtship. In pigeons and doves, the female often builds with material gathered by the male, or the male builds alone.
3) Nonstop babies. Thanks to crop milk, made by both parents (yes, the fathers too), pigeons can breed at any time of the year and lay eggs up to six times a year.
