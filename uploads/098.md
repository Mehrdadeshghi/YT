# Wiki Roulette #098 · Pickles — the dog who found the stolen World Cup (Football Stories · Part 1)
**Title:** This dog saved the World Cup 🐶🏆
**Alt:** The World Cup was stolen. A dog found it.

**Description:**
Hero dog or just lucky? Comment HERO or LUCKY 👇

On 20 March 1966, months before the World Cup in England, the Jules Rimet Trophy was stolen from a stamp exhibition at Westminster Central Hall in London. The thief demanded a £15,000 ransom. One week later, on 27 March, a four-year-old collie mix named Pickles sniffed at a package wrapped in newspaper next to a parked car on Beulah Hill in south London — inside was the trophy. England went on to win the tournament, and Pickles was invited to the victory banquet. He was given a medal and starred in the 1966 film "The Spy with a Cold Nose".
