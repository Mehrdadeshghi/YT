**Title:** This water is 400 °C. It doesn't boil.

Black smokers on the sea floor vent water from 60 °C up to 464 °C. It doesn't boil because of the enormous pressure at depth. Chimneys can grow huge: one called Godzilla reached 40 m before it fell over in 1996. Giant tube worms grow over 2 m tall with no mouth or gut — bacteria feed them, and the whole ecosystem runs on chemicals instead of sunlight. Some scientists think life on Earth may have begun at vents like these. Footage: NOAA and Ifremer.
