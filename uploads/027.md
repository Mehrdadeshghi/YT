# Wiki Roulette #027 · Violet Jessop
**Title:** She survived the Titanic. And its 2 sisters
**Alt:** The woman every ship disaster missed

**Description:**
Violet Jessop was a stewardess on RMS Olympic when it collided with the warship HMS Hawke in 1911. Seven months later she was aboard its sister ship, the Titanic; an officer handed her a baby to look after in lifeboat 16. In 1916 she served on the third sister, the hospital ship Britannic, which sank in the Aegean. She jumped from her lifeboat as the propellers shredded the boats around it. She lived to 83.
