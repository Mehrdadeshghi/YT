**Title:** She guarded her eggs for 4.5 years.
**Alt:** The octopus mother with the longest brooding record

**Description:**

A female deep-sea octopus (Graneledone boreopacifica) was watched by MBARI in Monterey Canyon brooding her eggs for 53 months — about 4.5 years, the longest known egg-brooding period of any animal. The eggs were never left unattended, and there is no evidence she ever fed again. Most octopuses live only 1–2 years. The footage shows the same species, filmed by NOAA in 2023 (not the brooding mother herself).
