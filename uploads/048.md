# Wiki Roulette #048 · Oymyakon
**Title:** -67.7°C. People live here.
**Alt:** The coldest town on Earth

**Description:**
Oymyakon in Siberia is the coldest permanently inhabited settlement on Earth. On 6 February 1933 its weather station recorded −67.7 °C. In January the average low is around −49 °C, and schools only close below −55 °C. About 500 people live there.
