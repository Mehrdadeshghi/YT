# #169 Long video — A Journey Down to the Titanic (16:9)

**Title:** Why the Titanic Is Disappearing: A Journey 12,500 Feet Down

**Description:**
We follow the Titanic from the night of April 14, 1912 all the way down to the wreck, 12,500 feet below the North Atlantic: how she broke in two, how the bow and the stern fell, how she was lost for 73 years and found in 1985, and why scientists think she may not last much longer. Rusticles, iron-eating bacteria, and the Titanic's own species, Halomonas titanicae. Real 1912 photos and newsreel footage, real NOAA photos of the wreck.
