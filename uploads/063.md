Kaliningrad Oblast is Russian territory between Poland and Lithuania on the Baltic Sea. Until 1945 it was German Königsberg in East Prussia – the home town of philosopher Immanuel Kant. After the Potsdam Agreement the Soviet Union took it, and in 1946 the city was renamed Kaliningrad. Since Lithuania's independence in 1990, it has had no land connection to the rest of Russia.
