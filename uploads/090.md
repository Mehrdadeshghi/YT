This man sold the Eiffel Tower. He didn't own it. In 1925, Victor Lustig posed as a French government official. He told scrap metal dealers the tower would be sold for scrap. One dealer, André Poisson, paid him. Poisson was too embarrassed to go to the police. So Lustig came back and tried to sell it again. He also sold a 'money printing box' that printed nothing. He even got money out of Al Capone.
