# Wiki Roulette #040 · Stanislav Petrov
**Title:** He saved the world. Then got reprimanded.
**Alt:** The false alarm that almost ended everything

**Description:**
On 26 September 1983, Stanislav Petrov was the duty officer at the command center of the Soviet Oko early-warning system when it reported a missile launched from the United States, then up to four more. Instead of escalating it, he judged it a false alarm. He was right: sunlight reflecting off high clouds had fooled the satellites. He was later reprimanded over improper documentation and reassigned. He received international awards decades later.
