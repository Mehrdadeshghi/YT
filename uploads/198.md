**Title:** Why the Golden Gate Bridge is orange (the Navy wanted stripes) 🌉
**Alt title:** 38 painters never stop painting this bridge #shorts

**Description:**
The Golden Gate Bridge is maintained by 38 painters, and the painting never stops: salt spray and fog keep attacking the steel. Its color isn't red — it's "International Orange". The U.S. Navy suggested painting it in black and yellow stripes so passing ships could see it. Consulting architect Irving Morrow picked orange instead, because it complements the natural surroundings and stands out in fog. The bridge's shade is even slightly lighter than the standard International Orange, to increase visibility for ships.
