**Description:**
Crater Lake in Oregon is the deepest lake in the United States (1,949 feet). No rivers flow in or out; only rain and snow fill it. Since at least 1896, a 30-foot tree trunk, the "Old Man of the Lake", has been bobbing upright in it, about 4 feet above the water. In the summer of 1938 it was tracked drifting at least 62.1 miles. In 1988, scientists tied it up during submarine research; the weather turned stormy and it started snowing in August. They released it, and soon after the weather cleared, which encouraged superstitions.
