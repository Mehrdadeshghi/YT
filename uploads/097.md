Four countries want this diamond. Britain keeps it. In 1849, after a war, Britain took it from the Sikh Empire by treaty. The ruler who had to hand it over was a child king. Today it sits in a crown in the Tower of London. India, Pakistan, Afghanistan and Iran have all claimed it.
