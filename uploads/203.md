# #203 REEL v2 (E8) — How the black box survives a crash (and why it's orange)

**Title:** The black box survives 3,400 G's, and it isn't black ✈️
**Alt title:** Your plane's black box isn't black. #shorts

**Description:**
A flight recorder must survive an impact of 3,400 g, roughly like hitting the ground at 310 mph, and fire of over 1,000 °C (1,830 °F). It is painted bright orange so investigators can find it in the wreckage, and it is usually mounted in the tail, where it is most likely to survive. If it sinks, its underwater locator beacon pings for up to 30 days, from as deep as 20,000 feet (6,000 m). So why not build the whole plane out of it? It would be too heavy to fly.
