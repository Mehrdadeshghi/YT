# #152 CIRCLES #2 — How many circles does it take to draw Texas?

**Title:** How many circles does it take to draw Texas? 🤠
**Alt title:** 1 circle misses Texas by 148 miles. 500 circles? #shorts

**Description:**
Spinning circles, each riding on the last one, can draw any shape (a Fourier series). With 1 circle, Texas is off by up to 148 miles. With 500 circles, it's off by just 1 mile. Watch the Panhandle, El Paso, Big Bend and the Rio Grande appear one by one.

Math: discrete Fourier transform of the outline of Texas (4,096 points, Albers equal-area projection). "Misses by" = the largest distance between the drawn curve and the real outline.
