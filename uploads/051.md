Challenger Deep in the Mariana Trench is the deepest known point in Earth's oceans, at about 10,935 m. Mount Everest (8,849 m) placed on its floor would still have more than 2 km of water above its summit. The pressure down there is over 1,000 times that at the surface, and only about 27 people have ever been to the bottom.
