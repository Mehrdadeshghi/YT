# #155 CIRCLES #4 — How many circles does it take to draw California?

**Title:** How many circles does it take to draw California? 🌉
**Alt title:** 1 circle misses California by 172 miles. 500 circles? #shorts

**Description:**
Spinning circles, each riding on the last one, can draw any shape (a Fourier series). With 1 circle, California is off by up to 172 miles. With 500 circles, it's off by just 1 mile. Watch Cape Mendocino, San Francisco, Big Sur and the Colorado River appear one by one.

Math: discrete Fourier transform of the outline of California (4,096 points, Albers equal-area projection). "Misses by" = the largest distance between the drawn curve and the real outline.
