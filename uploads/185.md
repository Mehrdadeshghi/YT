# #185 OCEAN — Not a jellyfish

**Title:** This is not a jellyfish. It's not even one animal 🎈
**Alt title:** The Portuguese man o' war: a colony of clones with a sail #shorts

**Description:**
The Portuguese man o' war looks like a jellyfish, but it is a siphonophore: a colony of small units called zooids. All of them grow from one single fertilized egg and are genetically identical, and each type has one job: hunting, digesting or reproducing. Its gas-filled float works as a sail, and the gas inside can be up to 13% carbon monoxide, produced by the animal itself. The tentacles are usually about 30 feet long and can reach over 100 feet. Floats come in left- and right-handed forms that the wind pushes in opposite directions, so at most half of a population is driven towards the coast. Washed up and dead, a man o' war can still sting just as painfully, for hours or even days.
