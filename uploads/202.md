**Title:** 860 volts, and it's not even an eel ⚡
**Alt title:** This fish makes 860 volts. It's not an eel. #shorts

**Description:**
The electric eel can deliver shocks of up to 860 volts, more than seven times a U.S. wall outlet (120 V). And it isn't a true eel at all: it's a South American knifefish. It even breathes air, gulping at the surface about every two minutes. Its secret is about 6,000 cells stacked in a row like tiny batteries. When threatened, it can leap out of the water to shock. In 1800, the explorer Alexander von Humboldt watched electric eels attack horses that had been driven into a pool; two horses drowned.
