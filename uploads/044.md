# Wiki Roulette #044 · Charles Osborne (hiccups)
**Title:** He hiccuped for 68 years
**Alt:** The longest hiccups ever

**Description:**
In 1922, Charles Osborne was preparing to slaughter a 350-pound hog when it collapsed on top of him. He started hiccuping and didn't stop for over 68 years, an estimated 430 million hiccups, earning a Guinness world record for the longest hiccup attack. In 1990 the hiccups finally stopped.
