**Title:** He jumped with $200,000 and was never found 🪂
**Alt title:** $200,000, four parachutes, one night jump. #shorts

**Description:**
On November 24, 1971, a man calling himself Dan Cooper hijacked Northwest Orient Flight 305, a Boeing 727, saying he had a bomb. In Seattle he released the passengers in exchange for $200,000 (10,000 twenty-dollar bills) and four parachutes. After takeoff he lowered the 727's rear airstair and parachuted into the night over the forests of southwest Washington. In 1980, an eight-year-old boy found $6,000 of the ransom on the bank of the Columbia River. The rest was never recovered, his identity is still unknown, and it remains the only documented unsolved hijacking in commercial aviation history.
