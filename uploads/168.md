# #168 FOOTBALL — He scored 3 in a World Cup final. And lost. (2022 World Cup final)

**Title:** He scored 3 goals in a World Cup final. And lost. 🤯
**Alt title:** The greatest World Cup final ever? Argentina 3-3 France, 2022 #shorts

**Description:**
World Cup final, Lusail, Qatar, 18 December 2022. Argentina led France 2-0 (Messi penalty, Di María), and France did not have an attempt on goal until the 80th minute. Then Kylian Mbappé scored twice in 97 seconds: 2-2. In extra time Messi made it 3-2, and Mbappé scored another penalty: 3-3, the second hat-trick ever in a men's World Cup final after Geoff Hurst in 1966. With 15 seconds of extra time left, Emiliano Martínez blocked Kolo Muani's shot with his leg. In the shoot-out Coman's penalty was saved and Tchouaméni missed, and Argentina won 4-2 on penalties — their third World Cup. France became the first team to score three goals in a World Cup final and lose.
