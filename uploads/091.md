Monopoly was invented by a woman. A man got rich from it. In 1904, Lizzie Magie patented 'The Landlord's Game.' Its goal: show how landlords get rich while tenants get poor. It even had a second set of rules where everyone wins. Charles Darrow learned a copy of the game, and in 1935 sold 'Monopoly' as his own. He became the first millionaire game designer in history. Parker Brothers bought Magie's patent for just $500. Within a year, they were making 20,000 sets every week.
