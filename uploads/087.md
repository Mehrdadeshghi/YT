Who should own Ötzi: Italy or Austria? Comment ITALY or AUSTRIA 👇

On 19 September 1991, German hikers Helmut and Erika Simon found a frozen man at about 3,210 m in the Ötztal Alps. A pneumatic drill damaged his hip during the recovery, and his bow was broken. He turned out to be about 5,300 years old. A new survey showed he lay 92.56 m inside Italy, so today he is in the South Tyrol Museum of Archaeology in Bolzano. In 2001, scans revealed an arrowhead in his left shoulder: he was murdered. The finders fought for 17 years over a reward; the first offer was about €5,200, the final settlement in 2008 was €150,000.
