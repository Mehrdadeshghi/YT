# Wiki Roulette #022 · Lake Nyos
**Title:** This lake killed 1,746 people
**Alt:** The lake that suffocated a valley

**Description:**
On 21 August 1986, Lake Nyos in Cameroon, a lake in an old volcanic crater, suddenly released the carbon dioxide dissolved in its deep water. A 100-metre column of water and foam rose from the surface, and up to 300,000 tons of CO₂, heavier than air, rolled down the valleys. 1,746 people and 3,500 livestock suffocated, some up to 25 km away. Afterwards the blue lake had turned deep red. Since 2001, pipes vent the gas from the bottom.
