On 1 September 1859 Richard Carrington saw a giant solar flare. The plasma reached Earth in about 17.6 hours. Auroras appeared as far south as Cuba, Hawaii and Colombia, telegraph systems failed, operators got shocks and pylons threw sparks — two operators even sent messages for two hours on aurora current alone. A similar storm today could cost the US $600 billion to $2.6 trillion. Footage: NASA SDO (2024 flare), auroras of the 2024–2025 storms.
