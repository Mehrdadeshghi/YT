**Title:** The ocean made a sound. What was it?
**Alt:** The Bloop: the ocean's loudest mystery

**Description:**
In 1997, NOAA's underwater microphones picked up a powerful sound in the South Pacific, off South America. Sensors up to 4,800 km apart all heard it. They named it the Bloop, and some wondered if something bigger than any whale was out there. By 2012 the answer was in: an icequake, Antarctic ice cracking and breaking apart.
