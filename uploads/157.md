**Description:**
The sea pig (Scotoplanes) is a deep-sea sea cucumber, only 4 to 6 inches long, and it breathes through its butt. It walks on leg-like tube feet that it pumps full of water, baby crabs hide underneath it to stay safe from predators, and it finds dead whales on the seafloor by smell. Herds of up to 600 live as deep as 16,400 feet, and if you bring one up to the surface, it falls apart.
