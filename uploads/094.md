Everyone laughed at the hot coffee lawsuit. Until they heard the facts. In 1992, 79-year-old Stella Liebeck spilled McDonald's coffee in her lap. She got third-degree burns, needed skin grafts, and spent 8 days in hospital. The coffee was served at up to 88 °C. She asked for 20,000 dollars for her bills. McDonald's offered $800. In court, it came out there had been over 700 burn reports before. The jury awarded $2.7 million in punitive damages. The judge cut it to $480,000, and they later settled in secret.
