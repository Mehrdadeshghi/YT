# Wiki Roulette #060 · Baarle-Hertog
**Title:** The border goes through his door
**Alt:** Baarle: Belgium inside the Netherlands

**Description:**
Would you live in a house split by a border? Yes or no? Comment YES or NO 👇

In Baarle, the Netherlands and Belgium are mixed like a puzzle: 22 Belgian exclaves (Baarle-Hertog) lie inside the Netherlands, and inside those sit 7 Dutch counter-exclaves. One border runs through the middle of a front door, so the house has two addresses (Loveren 2, Baarle-Hertog / Loveren 19, Baarle-Nassau). When Dutch closing times were earlier, restaurant guests could simply move to a table on the Belgian side.
