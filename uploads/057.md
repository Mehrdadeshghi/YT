# Wiki Roulette #057 · Falkland Islands sovereignty dispute
**Title:** Falklands: 300 years, 4 flags
**Alt:** The 300-year fight for the Falklands

**Description:**
Britain, or Argentina: who should own them? Comment BRITAIN or ARGENTINA 👇

The Falkland Islands have flown four flags. 1690: a British captain makes the first recorded landing. 1764: France founds the first settlement, Port Saint Louis; in 1765 Britain builds Port Egmont. France hands its colony to Spain, and by 1811 both Spain and Britain have withdrawn. 1820: the United Provinces of the Río de la Plata (Buenos Aires) claim the islands; in 1833 Britain returns. 1982: Argentina invades – the war lasts 74 days and kills 907 people. In the 2013 referendum, 99.8% of voters (92% turnout) choose to remain British. Argentina still claims the islands.
