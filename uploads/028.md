# Wiki Roulette #028 · Emu War
**Title:** Australia went to war with birds. And lost.
**Alt:** The army vs. 20,000 emus

**Description:**
In 1932 around 20,000 emus were eating crops in Western Australia, so the army sent Major Meredith with two Lewis machine guns and 10,000 rounds. By his own count, 9,860 rounds brought 986 kills, about 10 bullets per bird, and historians think even that was inflated. He said the birds 'can face machine guns with the invulnerability of tanks'. The army withdrew, and later requests for military help (1934, 1943, 1948) were refused.
