# #188 WHY? #3 — Why do zebras have stripes?

**Title:** Why do zebras have stripes? 🦓
**Alt title:** Scientists tested 3 ideas. Only one survived. Why do zebras have stripes? #shorts

**Description:**
Horses are plain. Zebras wear stripes. Why? Biologists have argued about it since at least the 19th century. Here is what the tests found:
1) Camouflage? Zebras graze out in the open and don't behave like hiding animals, and a 2016 study found that lions and hyenas don't appear to see the stripes from a certain distance, day or night.
2) Keeping cool? A 2018 experiment dressed water-filled metal barrels in horse, zebra and cattle hides: the zebra stripes had no effect on temperature. (Other studies disagree, so this one is still debated.)
3) Biting flies — the idea with the most evidence. Horse flies spread diseases that are deadly to horses and zebras. In a 2019 study, stripes stopped flies from landing, on zebras and on horses dressed in zebra-print coats. How exactly the stripes do it is still not fully clear.
