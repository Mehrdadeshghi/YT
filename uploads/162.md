# Episode 162 DEEP SEA — This crab is 12 feet wide (Japanese spider crab)

**Title:** This crab is 12 feet wide 🦀
**Alt title:** The Japanese spider crab has the biggest leg span on Earth #shorts

**Description:**
The Japanese spider crab has the biggest leg span of any arthropod on Earth: up to 12 feet, claw to claw. One museum specimen measures 12.5 feet across. It weighs up to 42 pounds, it glues sponges and living animals onto its shell as a disguise, and to grow it climbs out of its own shell, which takes about 100 minutes, with the legs coming out last.
