**Title:** One octopus. 18 disguises. 🐙
**Alt title:** This octopus turns into a sea snake when attacked (mimic octopus) #shorts

**Description:**
One octopus, up to 18 different animals. The mimic octopus was only discovered in 1998, off Sulawesi, Indonesia. It spreads its arms out wide like the spines of a lionfish. It hides six arms and stretches out two to look like a sea snake, and when damselfish harassed one, it turned into their predator: a banded sea snake. Its favorite disguise is a flatfish: in a 2008 study, a single octopus did it nearly 500 times in 5 days.
