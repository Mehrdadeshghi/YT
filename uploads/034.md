# Wiki Roulette #034 · Anatoli Bugorski
**Title:** He put his head in a particle accelerator
**Alt:** The man hit by a proton beam

**Description:**
On 3 June 1978, physicist Anatoli Bugorski was checking faulty equipment on the U-70 synchrotron in Protvino, USSR, when a proton beam passed through his head. He saw a flash 'brighter than a thousand suns' and felt no pain, though the reported local dose was 2,000 to 3,000 sieverts, far beyond what doctors considered survivable. The left half of his face was paralysed and he lost hearing in his left ear, but his intellect was intact. He finished his PhD and kept working as a physicist.
