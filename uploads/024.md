# Wiki Roulette #024 · Bobbit worm
**Title:** This worm is almost 3 meters long 😳
**Alt:** The sea floor's hidden ambush predator

**Description:**
The bobbit worm (Eunice aphroditois) buries its whole body in the sea floor and waits with only its antennae showing. When prey comes close, it strikes with sharp mandibles that can snap some prey in half. The longest one measured was 299 cm. Fossil burrows near Taiwan suggest they have hunted like this for 20 million years.
