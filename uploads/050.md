# Wiki Roulette #050 · Pale Blue Dot
**Title:** That tiny dot is Earth.
**Alt:** Earth from 6 billion km away

In 1990, Voyager 1 was about 6 billion km from home when, at Carl Sagan's request, NASA turned its camera back toward Earth. In the famous Pale Blue Dot photo, Earth takes up just 0.12 of a pixel. Shortly afterwards, Voyager's cameras were switched off for good.
