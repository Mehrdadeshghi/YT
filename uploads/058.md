Pheasant Island in the Bidasoa river between France and Spain is the world's smallest condominium. The Treaty of the Pyrenees was signed here in 1659. Since then it is Spanish from 1 February to 31 July and French from 1 August to 31 January. It is only about 200 m long and 40 m wide, and access is forbidden except on rare heritage days.
