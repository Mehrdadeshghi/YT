**Alt title:** The "world's ugliest animal" is a pressure injury: what a blobfish really looks like #shorts

**Description:**
In 2013 a blobfish was voted the ugliest animal in the world. But that famous face is an injury: the fish was pulled up from more than 3,000 feet, and the fast drop in pressure ruptured its cells (barotrauma). Blobfish live where the water pressure is 60 to 120 times higher than at the surface, and down there a blobfish looks like a normal tadpole-shaped fish. Its jelly-like body is slightly lighter than water, so it floats above the seafloor without swimming and eats food that drifts in front of it.
