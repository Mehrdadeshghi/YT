# #176 TITANIC (short from #169) — Something is eating the Titanic

**Title:** Something is eating the Titanic 🦠

**Description:**
Iron-eating bacteria are slowly turning the Titanic into rust. Their rust hangs from the wreck like icicles (Ballard called them rusticles), and they are estimated to eat about 400 pounds of the hull every day. One species, Halomonas titanicae, was named after the ship, and its discoverer estimates the wreck could collapse as soon as 2030.
