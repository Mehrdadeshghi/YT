**Title:** Bayern had already won… then THIS happened 😱

**Description:**

26 May 1999, Camp Nou: Bayern Munich led Manchester United 1–0 after Mario Basler's free kick in the 6th minute, then hit the post and the crossbar. UEFA president Lennart Johansson left his seat to present the trophy — ribbons were already tied on. In injury time, Teddy Sheringham equalised after 36 seconds, and moments later Ole Gunnar Solskjær scored the winner. Johansson: "The winners are crying and the losers are dancing."
