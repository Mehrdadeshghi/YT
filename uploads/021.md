## Beschreibung
When a whale dies, it sinks, often more than 1,000 meters into total darkness. First, hagfish and sleeper sharks strip up to 60 kg of flesh a day. Then Osedax, the bone-eating worms, dissolve the bones with acid. Finally, bacteria feed on the fat inside the bones for 50, maybe 100 years. Whaling may have cut the biomass of the deep sea by more than 30%. Researchers estimate there may be around 690,000 whale falls on the ocean floor at any one time.
