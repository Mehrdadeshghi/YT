# Wiki Roulette #031 · Great Molasses Flood
**Title:** A wave of molasses hit Boston
**Alt:** Boston's deadliest sticky wave

**Description:**
On 15 January 1919 a storage tank in Boston's North End holding about 8.7 million litres (2.3 million US gallons) of molasses burst. A wave up to 8 m high raced through the streets at around 56 km/h. As it cooled in the winter air it thickened and trapped people. 21 people died and 150 were injured. For decades, residents said the area still smelled of molasses on hot summer days.
