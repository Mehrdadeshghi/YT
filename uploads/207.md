**Description:**
In 1587, about 115 English colonists settled on Roanoke Island (today North Carolina). Governor John White sailed back to England for supplies, and the war with Spain kept him away until 1590. He landed on August 18, his granddaughter Virginia Dare's third birthday. The houses had been dismantled. "CRO" was carved into a tree and "CROATOAN" into a post of the palisade. They had agreed to carve their destination, and a cross pattée if they were in danger, so White took it as a peaceful move to Croatoan Island. Then the ship's anchor cable snapped and the search could not continue. What happened to the colonists has never been proven.
