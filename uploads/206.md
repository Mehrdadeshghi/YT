**Description:**
On October 28, 1965, the last piece of the 630-foot Gateway Arch in St. Louis, the keystone (10 short tons), was supposed to slide into an 8.5-foot gap at the top. But thermal expansion had squeezed the gap by 5 inches. Workers sprayed the south leg with fire hoses to cool it down and make it contract, and the keystone went in with only 6 inches to spare. For the next section, a hydraulic jack had to pry the legs six feet apart.
