# Wiki Roulette #037 · Anglo-Zanzibar War
**Title:** The shortest war ever: 38 minutes
**Alt:** This war ended before breakfast

**Description:**
On 27 August 1896, Britain gave Zanzibar's new sultan, Khalid bin Barghash, until 09:00 to leave the palace. He refused. At 09:00 British ships opened fire, and by about 09:40 it was over, around 38 to 45 minutes in total, the shortest recorded war in history. About 500 Zanzibari defenders were killed or wounded; one British sailor was wounded. Britain then imposed 300,000 rupees in reparations on the sultan's supporters, to cover the shells and the looting.
