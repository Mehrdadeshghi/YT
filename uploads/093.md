The Mona Lisa wasn't that famous. Until someone stole it. On 21 August 1911, Vincenzo Peruggia walked into the Louvre. He wore a white worker's smock, so nobody stopped him. He took the painting off the wall and walked out with it. Police even arrested the poet Apollinaire and questioned Pablo Picasso. Crowds came to stare at the empty wall. Two years later, he tried to sell it in Florence, and got caught. He said it belonged to Italy. He served just 7 months.
