**Title:** Two shrimp, locked in glass. Forever 💍

**Description:**
Venus' flower basket is a deep-sea sponge with a skeleton of glass. A pair of shrimp often gets in while young and grows too big to leave. In Japan it became a symbol of a couple staying together for life.
