# Wiki Roulette #064 · Neutral Moresnet
**Title:** A country made for one zinc mine
**Alt:** Neutral Moresnet: 3.5 km² of nowhere

**Description:**

In 1816 Prussia and the Netherlands could not agree who should get the valuable Vieille Montagne zinc mine, so they made the area neutral: Neutral Moresnet, about 3.5 km² and roughly triangular. In 1908 there was a plan to turn it into the first Esperanto-speaking state, Amikejo. In 1920 the Treaty of Versailles gave it to Belgium – today it is the municipality of Kelmis.
