# Wiki Roulette #041 · Kentucky meat shower
**Title:** In 1876, it rained meat
**Alt:** The day meat fell from the sky

**Description:**
On 3 March 1876, chunks of meat fell from the sky near Olympia Springs in Bath County, Kentucky, over an area of about 90 by 45 metres. Pieces were up to about 10 cm wide. Some people tasted it and guessed lamb or deer. Microscopic analysis later identified lung tissue from either a horse or a human infant. The leading explanation is vultures regurgitating while flying overhead.
