**Alt:** Who wins the race? 🐢 or 🐇

**Description:**
Who is faster — a super speedy hare or a slow little tortoise? 🐢🐇 A short bedtime story for kids about a famous race!

The hare loves to show off. "You're so slow, Tortoise!" So the tortoise says: "Let's have a race!" The hare zooms away... and takes a little nap under a tree. Step by step, the tortoise keeps walking. Who crosses the finish line first? 🏁

Moral: Slow and steady wins the race — never give up!

This is a retelling of "The Tortoise and the Hare", one of Aesop's Fables (ancient Greece), told in our own words. All characters and pictures are original drawings.
