**Description:**
Can a little monkey trick a big, hungry crocodile? 🐒🐊 A short bedtime story for kids about friendship, apples and thinking fast!

Once upon a time, a clever monkey lived in an apple tree on a beautiful island. He shared his apples with a hungry crocodile — until the crocodile's wife wanted the monkey's heart... How does the monkey escape? Watch till the end! 🍎

Moral: Stay calm and think fast — and never trust a hungry crocodile!

This is a retelling of "The Monkey and the Crocodile", a very old fable from India (Panchatantra / Jataka tales), told in our own words. All characters and pictures are original drawings.
