# Wiki Roulette #106 · Brazil 1–7 Germany, 2014 World Cup semi-final (Football's Craziest Moments #6)
**Title:** Brazil 0–5. After 29 minutes. 😳⚽
**Alt:** 4 goals in 6 minutes — at Brazil's home World Cup

**Description:**
Worst defeat in football history? Comment YES or NO 👇

8 July 2014, Mineirão, Belo Horizonte: the World Cup semi-final in Brazil's own tournament. Brazil had not lost a competitive home match since 1975 — 62 games. But Neymar was out with a fractured vertebra and captain Thiago Silva was suspended. Thomas Müller scored after 11 minutes, Miroslav Klose made it 2–0 in the 23rd minute — his 16th World Cup goal, a new record ahead of Ronaldo's 15. Then Toni Kroos scored twice in 69 seconds and Sami Khedira added a fifth: four goals in six minutes, 5–0 after 29 minutes. Many fans were in tears and many left at half-time. André Schürrle scored twice more, and the remaining Brazil fans gave Germany a standing ovation. Oscar's late goal made it 7–1 — the biggest winning margin in a World Cup semi-final. Five days later Germany won the World Cup.
