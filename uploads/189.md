# #189 WHY? — Why do onions make you cry?

**Title:** Why do onions make you cry? 🧅😢
**Alt title:** A whole onion is harmless. Cut it and you cry. Why? #shorts

**Description:**
A whole onion does nothing to you. Cut it, and your eyes sting and water. Three reasons:
1) It's a trap. The onion keeps enzymes called alliinases locked away inside its cells. When an animal bites it — or your knife cuts it — the cells break and the enzymes are released. It's part of the plant's defence against hungry animals.
2) Tear gas. The alliinases make sulfenic acids, and a second enzyme, lachrymatory factor synthase, turns one of them into syn-propanethial-S-oxide: a gas that drifts through the air and reaches your eyes.
3) Your eyes fight back. The gas activates sensory nerves in the eye, and your tear glands flood it out. Irritation can raise tear production up to about 100 times.
The fix: cut onions under running water or in a bowl of water, and leave the root end on — the base holds the most sulphur compounds.
Bonus: tearless onions exist. "Sunions", bred over 30 years without genetic modification, went on sale in 2018.
