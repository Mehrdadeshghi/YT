# #153 HOW IT WORKS #11 — How does a missile find its target?

**Title:** How does a missile find its target? 🎯
**Alt title:** No pilot. No eyes. Still on target. Here's how. #shorts

**Description:**
No pilot, no eyes, yet it finds the target. Inside: gyroscopes and accelerometers that feel every turn (inertial navigation). But even the best sensors drift about 164 feet in 17 minutes, so it checks GPS, whose signal travels over 12,000 miles and arrives weak enough to be jammed. Plan B: a radar matches the hills below to a stored map (TERCOM), and near the target a camera compares the ground with satellite photos (DSMAC). And yes, they do miss: for GPS-guided bombs, 33 feet is the circle where only half of them land (CEP).
