# Wiki Roulette #046 · Tanganyika laughter epidemic
**Title:** A laughing epidemic closed 14 schools
**Alt:** The laughter that wouldn't stop

**Description:**
On 30 January 1962, at a girls' boarding school in Kashasha in what is now Tanzania, uncontrollable laughter broke out; 95 of the 159 pupils were affected. It spread to nearby villages, affecting around 1,000 people, and 14 schools were shut down. It lasted about 18 months. Experts attribute it to mass psychogenic illness.
