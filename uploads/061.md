The Diomede Islands in the Bering Strait are about 3.8 km apart. Big Diomede belongs to Russia, Little Diomede to the USA, and the International Date Line runs between them – so Big Diomede is 21 hours ahead (20 in summer). They are called Tomorrow Island and Yesterday Island. In 1987 Lynne Cox swam from Little Diomede to Big Diomede.
