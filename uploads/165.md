# #165 OCEAN — 24 eyes. No brain.

**Title:** 24 eyes. No brain. 👁️
**Alt title:** The box jellyfish has 24 eyes and no brain, and it hunts #shorts

**Description:**
A box jellyfish has no true brain, but it has 24 eyes: six on each of its four sides. Two eyes in each cluster are real lens eyes, with a lens, cornea and retina. In species like Tripedalia cystophora, the upper lens eye looks straight up out of the water, and the jellyfish uses the tree canopy above to steer to its mangrove lagoons. Box jellyfish don't just drift: they hunt small fish and swim at up to 4.6 mph. More than 170 toxic proteins have been found in their venom, and in Australia jugs of vinegar are placed along swimming beaches for rapid first aid.
