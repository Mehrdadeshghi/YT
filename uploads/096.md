The diamond engagement ring? That idea was sold to you. In 1947, copywriter Frances Gerety wrote four words for De Beers. 'A diamond is forever.' In 2000, Advertising Age called it the best slogan of the 20th century. At its peak, De Beers controlled about 90% of the world's diamond production. It bought up and stockpiled diamonds to keep them scarce. Today, lab-grown diamonds cost about 90% less. In 2025, De Beers even shut down its own lab-grown brand.
