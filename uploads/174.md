**Title:** The Titanic hit the seabed at 23 mph 🚢

**Description:**
After it broke in two, the bow of the Titanic hit the bottom of the North Atlantic at about 23 mph and dug about 60 feet into the mud. The stern had it worse: trapped air pockets imploded on the way down, and its decks collapsed on top of each other.
