**Description:**
About 150 people lived in Monowi, Nebraska in the 1930s. Since her husband Rudy died in 2004, Elsie Eiler has been the only resident. She is the mayor, clerk, treasurer and librarian, issued herself a liquor license for the Monowi Tavern, keeps Rudy's Library of about 5,000 books, and files an annual road plan so the town keeps its streetlights on.
