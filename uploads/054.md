# Wiki Roulette #054 · Darvaza gas crater
**Title:** This hole has burned since 1971
**Alt:** The Door to Hell

**Description:**
The Darvaza gas crater in Turkmenistan's Karakum Desert – nicknamed the Door to Hell – is about 70 m wide and 30 m deep. It was set on fire to burn off the natural gas and was expected to go out quickly. It never did. In 2013, explorer George Kourounis became the first person to reach its bottom. Now the authorities are trying to put it out.
