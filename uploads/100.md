9 July 2006, Olympiastadion Berlin. In the last match of his career, Zinedine Zidane put France ahead after 7 minutes with a chipped "Panenka" penalty. Marco Materazzi equalised for Italy. In the 109th minute of extra time, Materazzi tugged Zidane's shirt and insulted his sister — Zidane turned around and headbutted him in the chest. The referee only learned about it from the fourth official: red card. Italy won 5–3 on penalties, and Zidane was still awarded the Golden Ball as the tournament's best player.
