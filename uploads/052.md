# Wiki Roulette #052 · Tunguska event
**Title:** A forest flattened. No crater.
**Alt:** The Tunguska mystery of 1908

**Description:**
On 30 June 1908 a huge explosion hit the Tunguska region of Siberia. About 80 million trees across 2,150 km² were flattened, and people more than 60 km away were knocked off their feet. Yet there was no crater: the object, most likely a meteoroid, exploded in the air at about 5–10 km altitude.
