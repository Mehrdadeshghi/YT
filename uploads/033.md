# Wiki Roulette #033 · Cadaver Synod
**Title:** A pope put a dead pope on trial
**Alt:** The strangest trial in history

**Description:**
In January 897, Pope Stephen VI had his predecessor Pope Formosus, dead for about nine months, exhumed and propped on a throne in papal vestments, with a deacon answering for him. The corpse was found guilty and his papacy declared null and void. Three fingers of his right hand were cut off and the body thrown into the Tiber. Rome turned against Stephen, who was deposed and strangled in prison that summer.
