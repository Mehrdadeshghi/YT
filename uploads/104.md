**Description:**
Luck or genius? Comment LUCK or GENIUS 👇

Champions League semi-final 2012. Chelsea won the first leg 1–0 at Stamford Bridge (Drogba, 45+2'). In the second leg at Camp Nou, Busquets (35') and Iniesta (38') put Barcelona 2–0 up — and captain John Terry was sent off (36') for kneeing Alexis Sánchez. Ramires scored just before half-time, putting Chelsea ahead on away goals. In the second half Lionel Messi hit the crossbar with a penalty. In stoppage time Fernando Torres made it 2–2: Chelsea won 3–2 on aggregate and reached the final.
