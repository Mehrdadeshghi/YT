# #166 DEEP SEA — This crab grows its own food (yeti crab)

**Title:** This crab grows its own food. On its arms. 🦀
**Alt title:** The yeti crab farms bacteria on its hairy arms #shorts

**Description:**
The yeti crab (Kiwa hirsuta) was discovered in 2005, 7,200 feet down on hydrothermal vents in the South Pacific, south of Easter Island. Its hairy arms are covered in bacteria, and those bacteria are its main food. One species, Kiwa puravida, even "dances": it waves its claws to stir the water so its bacteria get the chemicals they need. Its eyes have no pigment, and it is thought to be blind.
