# Wiki Roulette #029 · Frank Hayes (jockey)
**Title:** He won his first race. Already dead.
**Alt:** The jockey who won after he died

**Description:**
On 4 June 1923 at Belmont Park, New York, 22-year-old jockey Frank Hayes rode Sweet Kiss, a 20-to-1 outsider. He had never won a race. Late in the race he suffered a heart attack, but his body stayed in the saddle and Sweet Kiss crossed the line first. Nobody noticed until they came to congratulate him. The horse never raced again and was said to be nicknamed 'Sweet Kiss of Death'.
