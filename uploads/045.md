# Wiki Roulette #045 · Kowloon Walled City
**Title:** 33,000 people. 2.6 hectares.
**Alt:** The densest place ever lived in

**Description:**
Kowloon Walled City in Hong Kong covered just 2.6 hectares (6.4 acres), smaller than four football fields, yet by 1987 about 33,000 people lived there, roughly 1,255,000 people per square kilometre. Around 300 interconnected buildings rose 13 to 14 storeys, capped by flight paths of the nearby airport. With almost no government control, unlicensed doctors and dentists practised freely. It was demolished between 1993 and 1994 and is now a park.
