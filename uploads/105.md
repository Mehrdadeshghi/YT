# Wiki Roulette #105 · Champions League final 2012, Munich (Chelsea 2012 · Part 2 of 2)

**Alt:** Bayern lost the final in their OWN stadium

**Description:**
Luckiest win in Champions League history? Comment YES or NO 👇

19 May 2012, Allianz Arena: the Champions League final in Bayern's own stadium — "Finale dahoam". Chelsea were missing four suspended players, including captain John Terry. Bayern had 20 corners, Chelsea one. Thomas Müller scored in the 83rd minute; in the 88th minute Chelsea won their first corner of the match, Juan Mata swung it in and Didier Drogba headed the equaliser. In extra time Drogba fouled Franck Ribéry — Arjen Robben's penalty was saved by Petr Čech. In the shoot-out Čech saved from Olić and tipped Schweinsteiger's kick onto the post, and Drogba scored the decisive last penalty: Chelsea's first Champions League title. Even the G8 leaders at Camp David watched the shoot-out.
