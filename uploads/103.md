7 July 1974, Olympiastadion Munich. With just a minute gone, Johan Cruyff was brought down by Uli Hoeneß in the German area after a solo run — and Johan Neeskens scored the penalty before any German player had touched the ball. In the 25th minute West Germany got a penalty of their own: Paul Breitner, 1–1. In the 43rd minute Gerd Müller scored the winner. West Germany won 2–1 — the first World Cup final with penalties.
