**Title:** These fake heads fooled Alcatraz guards all night 🧠
**Alt title:** The 1962 Alcatraz escape: no bodies were ever found #shorts

**Description:**
On the night of June 11, 1962, Frank Morris and the brothers John and Clarence Anglin escaped from Alcatraz. In their beds they left dummy heads made from soap, toilet paper and hair from the barbershop floor. For six months they had dug through the vents under their sinks with spoons and a drill made from a vacuum cleaner motor. Then they paddled into the freezing bay on a homemade raft, six by fourteen feet. The escape was only discovered at 7 a.m., when a guard tried to wake Clarence Anglin and the head fell on the floor. No bodies were ever found. The FBI concluded in 1979 that they most likely drowned, but the debate never ended.
