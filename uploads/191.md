# #191 WHY? #6 — Why do you never see baby pigeons?

**Title:** Why do you never see baby pigeons? 🐣
**Alt title:** 120 million pigeons. Zero babies? Here's why. #shorts

**Description:**
There are up to 120 million pigeons worldwide, yet almost nobody has seen a baby one. Three reasons:
1) Hidden nests. Wild rock doves nest in crevices of cliffs and caves, on a ledge, under cover. In cities, skyscrapers, highway overpasses and other buildings with crevices play the cliff.
2) They stay home. Pigeon chicks ("squabs") are fed by both parents until they are about four weeks old, first on crop milk ("pigeon milk", made by mom AND dad). Their body weight doubles within the first two days. They leave the nest after 25–32 days.
3) They leave adult-sized. Squabs reach adult size after roughly a month, so the "new" pigeons you see already look grown-up.
