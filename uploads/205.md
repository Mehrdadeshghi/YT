# #205 REEL v2 (E8) — The year America switched off Niagara Falls (1969)

**Title:** In 1969, America switched off its side of Niagara Falls 💧
**Alt title:** The American Falls ran dry in 1969. Here's why. #shorts

**Description:**
In June 1969, the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers built a temporary dam of rock and earth and completely diverted the Niagara River away from the American Falls, the U.S. side of Niagara Falls. The Horseshoe Falls took all the extra water. Rockfalls had piled a huge mound of rock at the base, so engineers studied the riverbed and bolted and strengthened weak spots. Two bodies were found under the dry falls. In November 1969 the temporary dam was blown up with dynamite, and the water came back.
