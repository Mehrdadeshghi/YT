**Description:**
The frilled shark (Chlamydoselachus anguineus) can be pregnant for up to three and a half years. It is named after the frilly look of its six pairs of gill slits, its mouth holds 300 needle-like, trident-shaped teeth, and it can swallow prey more than half its own size whole. Scientists call it a living fossil. The first known photo of one alive in its deep-water habitat was taken by NOAA in 2004, 2,866 feet down.
