Giant isopods have survived more than 5 years without food in captivity. This real NOAA footage was filmed by the robot sub Deep Discoverer at 816 m in the Gulf of Mexico. They live as deep as 2,140 m, scavenge dead whales, fish and squid, reach up to about 50 cm, and each eye has nearly 4,000 facets. Their relatives are the woodlice in your garden.
