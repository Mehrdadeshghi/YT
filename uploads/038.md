# Wiki Roulette #038 · Hachikō
**Title:** He waited 9 years for his owner
**Alt:** The most loyal dog in history

**Description:**
Hachikō met his owner, Professor Hidesaburō Ueno, at Shibuya Station in Tokyo every day. In May 1925 Ueno died suddenly and never came back. Hachikō kept returning to the station almost every day for nine years and nine months. In 1934 a bronze statue of him was unveiled at Shibuya Station, while he was still alive. He died in 1935.
