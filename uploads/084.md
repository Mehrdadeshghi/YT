Instagram says there is no single algorithm: Feed, Stories, Explore and Reels each rank content their own way, using thousands of signals to predict whether you'll watch, like, share or save a post. Head of Instagram Adam Mosseri named the top three signals (Jan 2025): watch time, likes and sends — likes matter a bit more with followers, sends matter more for reaching non-followers. Since 2022 Instagram recommends reposts and aggregator accounts less, and watermarks, low resolution or mostly-text videos can get less reach. Trial Reels (Dec 2024) let creators test a Reel on non-followers first.
