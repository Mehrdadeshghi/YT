# Wiki Roulette #078 · Turritopsis dohrnii — the jellyfish that ages backwards (Bizarre Nature · Part 1)
**Title:** This animal ages backwards 🪼
**Alt:** The jellyfish that can't die of old age

**Description:**
Would you want to live forever? Comment YES or NO 👇

Turritopsis dohrnii is a jellyfish only about 4.5 mm wide. When it's injured, sick or old, it can turn back into a polyp — its baby stage — and grow up all over again. In one lab, a colony did this 11 times in two years. That's why it's called "biologically immortal". But in the wild, most still get eaten or get sick.
