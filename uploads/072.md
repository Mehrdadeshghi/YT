The ocean sunfish produces more eggs than any other known vertebrate: up to 300 million at a time. Its larvae are just millimetres long, yet it can grow up to 60 million times heavier. Adults weigh up to about 1,000 kg, often bask at the surface (possibly to warm up after deep, cold dives) and carry more than 40 kinds of parasites.
