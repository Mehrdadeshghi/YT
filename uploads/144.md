# #144 CIRCLES #1 — How many circles does it take to draw the USA?

**Title:** How many circles does it take to draw the USA? 🇺🇸
**Alt title:** 1 circle misses the USA by 812 miles. 500 circles? #shorts

**Description:**
Spinning circles, each riding on the last one, can draw any shape (a Fourier series). With 1 circle, the USA is off by up to 812 miles. With 500 circles, it's off by just 8 miles. Watch Florida, Texas, Michigan and Cape Cod appear one by one.

Math: discrete Fourier transform of the outline of the contiguous United States (4,096 points, Albers equal-area projection). "Misses by" = the largest distance between the drawn curve and the real outline.
