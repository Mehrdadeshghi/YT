# Wiki Roulette #047 · Michel Lotito
**Title:** He ate an airplane. Reportedly.
**Alt:** The man who ate metal

**Description:**
Michel Lotito, known as Monsieur Mangetout ('Mister Eats-All'), had pica. He is said to have eaten a whole Cessna 150 between 1978 and 1980, plus 18 bicycles, 7 television sets and a coffin, nearly nine tonnes of metal between 1959 and 1997. Guinness World Records gave him a brass plaque for the strangest diet, which he ate too. (A 2022 fact-check could not confirm the entire aircraft.) Don't try this.
