22 June 1986, Estadio Azteca, Mexico City: Argentina vs England in the World Cup quarter-final, four years after the Falklands War. In the 51st minute, Diego Maradona jumped with England goalkeeper Peter Shilton and knocked the ball in with his left hand. The referee said he did not see it — goal. Afterwards Maradona said it was scored "a little with the head of Maradona and a little with the hand of God". Four minutes later he ran 60 yards past four English outfield players and scored the "Goal of the Century". Argentina won 2–1 and went on to win the World Cup.
