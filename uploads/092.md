She's over 3,000 years old, and Egypt wants her back. In 1912, German archaeologists found this bust in Amarna, Egypt. In 1913, the finds were split, and the bust went to Germany. Egypt says the team hid how valuable it was. It was kept out of sight until 1924. Egypt has demanded it back ever since. Even Hitler refused, saying he would never give up the head of the Queen. Today, about 500,000 people a year see her in Berlin.
