# Wiki Roulette #110 · HOW IT WORKS #2: How GPS works (and why it needs Einstein)
**Title:** Your phone uses Einstein. Every day. 🛰️🤯
**Alt:** Without Einstein your map would be 10 km off — per day

**Description:**
Did you know your map app needs Einstein? Comment YES or NO 👇

GPS satellites orbit about 20,000 km above us, each with an atomic clock on board. They constantly broadcast the exact time and their position. The signal needs roughly 0.07 seconds to reach your phone — and from that delay your phone calculates the distance. One satellite puts you somewhere on a circle, two leave two possible points, the third pins you down (a fourth corrects your phone's own clock). The twist: in orbit, time runs faster. Weaker gravity makes the satellite clocks gain about 45 microseconds per day, their speed makes them lose about 7 — net about 38 microseconds per day. Without correcting for Einstein's relativity, positions would drift by about 10 km every day.
