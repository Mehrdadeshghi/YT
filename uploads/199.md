**Title:** The room behind Lincoln's head 🗿
**Alt title:** Mount Rushmore has a hidden Hall of Records (and you can't go in) #shorts

**Description:**
Behind Abraham Lincoln's head on Mount Rushmore there is an unfinished room. Sculptor Gutzon Borglum planned a grand "Hall of Records": a vault cut into the mountain to protect and show America's greatest documents, including the Declaration of Independence and the Constitution, reached by a long series of stairways up the side of the mountain. His workers cut about 70 feet (21 m) into the rock in a crag behind the Lincoln figure, but work stopped in 1939 to focus on the faces, and no trail was ever built. The vault is off-limits to the public.
In 1998 a repository was placed inside the mouth of the cave: 16 enamel panels with the history of Mount Rushmore and the texts of the documents Borglum wanted to keep there, in a teakwood box inside a titanium vault, under an engraved granite capstone.
