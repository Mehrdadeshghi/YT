Pineapple on pizza? Comment YES or NO 👇

Pineapple on pizza wasn't invented in Hawaii. Or in Italy. It was invented in Canada, in 1962. By Sam Panopoulos, a Greek-born cook running a restaurant in Ontario. Inspired by sweet and sour Chinese dishes, he tried pineapple. The name? It came from the brand of canned pineapple. At first, customers didn't like it. By 1999, it was Australia's most popular pizza. In 2017, Iceland's president joked he would ban it. Today, 12% of American pizza eaters love it, and 24% hate it.
