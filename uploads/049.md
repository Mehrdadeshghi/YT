**Title:** The loudest sound in history
**Alt:** Krakatoa: heard 4,800 km away

**Description:**

In August 1883 the volcano Krakatoa in Indonesia exploded. The sound was heard on Rodrigues Island, about 4,800 km away, where people thought it was cannon fire. The pressure wave circled the Earth more than three times, tsunamis reached up to 42 m, and at least 36,000 people died. For months afterwards, sunsets around the world glowed red.
