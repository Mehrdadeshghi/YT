# #175 TITANIC (short from #169) — The craziest plans to raise the Titanic

**Title:** They wanted to raise the Titanic with ping-pong balls 🏓

**Description:**
People really proposed raising the Titanic: fill the wreck with ping-pong balls (they would be crushed long before reaching it), or freeze it inside an iceberg made with half a million tons of liquid nitrogen. At 12,500 feet the pressure is over 6,000 psi. None of the plans ever worked.
