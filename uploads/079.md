In 2007, dried-out tardigrades (water bears) spent 10 days exposed to open space on the FOTON-M3 mission — some came back to life. They are only about 0.5 mm long, live in moss and soil, and survive by drying into a 'tun' for years. They have survived −272 °C to +149 °C. In 2019 a capsule of dried tardigrades crashed on the Moon aboard the Israeli lander Beresheet.
