# #177 OCEAN — Its slime grows 10,000× in 0.4 seconds (hagfish)

**Title:** This slime grows 10,000× in 0.4 seconds 🤢
**Alt title:** The hagfish: no jaws, just slime, and it ties itself in a knot #shorts

**Description:**
Hagfish are eel-shaped, jawless fish, also called "slime eels". When their slime hits seawater it expands to 10,000 times its original size in 0.4 seconds. If a predator fish bites, the slime clogs its gills and it lets the hagfish go. When the hagfish is covered in its own slime, it ties itself in an overhand knot and slides the knot from head to tail to scrape the slime off. In 2017 a road accident on U.S. Highway 101 spilled 7,500 pounds of hagfish, and they made enough slime to cover the road and a nearby car. And the leather sold as "eel skin" for wallets and belts usually comes from hagfish.
