|---|---|

| Loose objects become projectiles; heavy fog fills the cabin | *Uncontrolled decompression* |

## 3 · Inside a lightning strike
| Line | Source |
|---|---|
| Ice crystals (+) rise, graupel/soft hail (−) stays lower; positive on top, negative below | *Lightning* (charge separation) |
| Stepped leader: ~90 % of steps ≈ 45 m (148 ft) | *Lightning* (leaders) |
| Upward streamer from grounded objects / tall trees | *Lightning* |
| Return stroke from the attachment point up the channel at up to 1/3 the speed of light — the most luminous part ("the flash you see shoots up") | *Lightning* (return stroke) |
| Average negative bolt 30 kA | *Lightning* |
| Air heats to about 30,000 °C (54,000 °F) | *Lightning* (lead) |
| Sun's surface: effective temperature 5,772 K = 5,499 °C ≈ 9,930 °F → 54,000 / 9,930 ≈ 5.4× ("five times hotter") | *Photosphere* |
| Shock wave = thunder | *Lightning* (thunder) |
| Re-strikes typically 40–50 ms apart, usually re-using the channel → "strobe light" flicker | *Lightning* |
